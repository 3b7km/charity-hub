# Donor Guide

Welcome to CharityHub. This guide explains how to manage your donations, download certificates, and handle your account data.

## Making a Donation
1. Navigate to the **Campaigns** page and select a campaign you wish to support.
2. Enter your donation amount and select either **One-time** or **Monthly**.
3. You will be redirected to the secure Stripe checkout. We do not store your credit card information.

## Downloading Certificates
After a successful donation, you will receive an email containing a cryptographically verifiable PDF certificate. 
You can also download this at any time by:
1. Logging into your account.
2. Navigating to the **My Donations** dashboard.
3. Clicking **Download Certificate** next to any completed donation.

## Managing Subscriptions
If you set up a monthly donation, you can cancel it via the **My Subscriptions** page.
1. Locate the active subscription.
2. Click **Cancel Subscription**. Your card will no longer be billed.

## Requesting Data Deletion
Under GDPR, you have the right to request the erasure of your personal data. 
Please contact `privacy@charityhub.com`. Your personal data will be anonymised within 30 days. Financial records are retained for 7 years for tax compliance, but they will be disconnected from your identity.
